---
title: "Carrots with cumin"
dek: "The salad on every Moroccan lunch table, soft, sharp and smelling of cumin."
date: 2026-10-25
tint: "#E07B39"
hour: midday
serves: "4"
time: "25 minutes"
blends: [kemoun, chermoula]
related: [cumin, bread-round-the-plate, the-second-helping]
ingredients:
  - 600 g carrots, peeled and cut into thick coins
  - 2 garlic cloves, whole
  - 4 tablespoons olive oil
  - 1 tablespoon lemon juice or a splash of vinegar
  - 1 teaspoon kemoun
  - 1 teaspoon chermoula (optional)
  - A handful of chopped parsley or coriander
steps:
  - Boil the carrots with the garlic in salted water until tender but not falling apart, about 10 to 12 minutes. Drain.
  - Mash the garlic and whisk it with the olive oil, lemon, [[kemoun]] and, if you like a little heat, the [[chermoula]].
  - Toss the warm carrots in the dressing so they drink it in. Leave them for at least ten minutes.
  - Scatter the herbs over and serve at room temperature, with bread.
---
Every Moroccan lunch starts with small salads, and this one is always there. It is the dish that proves cumin and carrots were made for each other.

It tastes better after an hour, even better the next day, and best of all wiped up with bread straight from the bowl.
