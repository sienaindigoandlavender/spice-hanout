---
title: "Lamb with prunes and almonds"
dek: "The tagine for celebrations, sweet, slow and shamelessly rich."
date: 2026-10-24
tint: "#5C2A1E"
hour: evening
serves: "4 to 6"
time: "2 hours 30 minutes"
blends: [sweet-tagine, ras-el-hanout, taliouine-saffron]
related: [saffron, cinnamon, head-of-the-shop]
ingredients:
  - 1.2 kg lamb shoulder, in large pieces
  - 2 onions, grated
  - 4 tablespoons olive oil
  - 2 teaspoons Sweet Tagine
  - 1 teaspoon Ras el Hanout
  - A pinch of saffron threads, soaked in a spoon of warm water
  - 250 g prunes
  - 2 tablespoons honey
  - 100 g blanched almonds, fried golden
  - Toasted sesame seeds, to finish
steps:
  - In a heavy pot or tagine, warm the oil and stir in the onions, [[sweet-tagine|Sweet Tagine]] and [[ras-el-hanout|Ras el Hanout]]. Add the lamb and turn it until coated.
  - Add the [[taliouine-saffron|saffron]] and its water and enough water to come halfway up the meat. Cover and cook very gently for about two hours, until the meat is soft enough to pull apart with bread.
  - Meanwhile simmer the prunes in a little water with the honey until glossy, about 15 minutes.
  - Lift the lid, let the sauce thicken for a few minutes, then lay the prunes over the meat and pour on their syrup.
  - Scatter with the fried almonds and sesame and bring the whole pot to the table.
---
This is the tagine of weddings and feast days, the one that makes a house smell like an occasion. Sweet against savoury, soft against crunch, and a sauce that demands a second loaf.

It cannot be hurried and it should not be. Put it on, go and do something else, come back to a kitchen that smells of saffron and cinnamon.
