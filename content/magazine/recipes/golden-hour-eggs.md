---
title: "Golden Hour eggs"
dek: "Soft eggs in a small clay dish, olive oil, warm bread. A breakfast that refuses to rush."
date: 2026-10-26
tint: "#F2C14E"
hour: morning
serves: "2"
time: "10 minutes"
blends: [golden-hour, kemoun]
related: [three-meals, turmeric, bread-round-the-plate]
ingredients:
  - 4 eggs
  - 3 tablespoons olive oil
  - 1 teaspoon Golden Hour
  - 1 ripe tomato, grated (optional)
  - A pinch of kemoun to finish
  - Warm bread, to scoop
steps:
  - Warm the olive oil in a small pan or a clay dish over a gentle flame. Stir in the [[golden-hour|Golden Hour]] and let it sizzle for a few seconds, until it smells warm.
  - If you like, add the grated tomato and cook it down for three or four minutes.
  - Crack in the eggs. Cover and cook on low until the whites are just set and the yolks still soft.
  - Finish with a pinch of [[kemoun]]. Bring the dish to the table and eat it straight from the pan with bread.
---
This is the breakfast you make when nobody has to be anywhere. The eggs cook in the same dish they are eaten from, the bread does the rest, and the spice turns the oil the colour of the first sun on a wall.

Eat it slowly. Make tea. Wipe the dish clean.
