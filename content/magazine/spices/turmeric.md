Every evening in Ramadan, pots of harira simmer across Morocco, and they are all the same warm yellow. That colour is turmeric.

*Kharkoum* travelled to Morocco from India along the same routes as pepper and ginger, and it never left. It is the quiet base of countless tagines, the colour of chicken with preserved lemon, the glow in the broth of a Friday couscous. It tastes earthy and a little bitter on its own; in a pot it holds everything together.

Turmeric likes fat and it likes black pepper. Cooks never needed a reason for that pairing; it simply tastes finished. That is why our [[golden-hour]] blend puts them together, with ginger and cinnamon, for warm milk and morning eggs.
